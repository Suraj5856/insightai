Acme Tech Employee Handbook

Working Hours
Office hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday to Friday. Employees can work from home on Wednesdays and Fridays. Core collaboration hours are 11 AM to 4 PM.

Leave Policy
Every employee gets 18 paid leaves per year. Sick leave is separate and gives 10 additional days per year. Unused paid leaves up to 6 days can be carried forward to the next year. Leave requests must be submitted at least 3 days in advance through the HR portal, except for emergencies.

Probation
The probation period is 6 months. During probation, employees are eligible for 1 paid leave per month. Performance is reviewed at the end of month 3 and month 6.

Learning Budget
Each employee receives an annual learning budget of 25,000 rupees for courses, books and certifications. Claims must be submitted with receipts within 30 days of purchase.

IT Support
For laptop or software problems, email it-help@acmetech.example or raise a ticket on the helpdesk portal. Typical response time is 4 working hours.
